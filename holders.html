Never moved since the migration

At the 30 October 2024 migration, every holder's RIO on BNB Chain and Ethereum was re-issued on a new contract. Two measures track what has happened to it since, one inside the other. Updated weekly.

RIO never moved: wallets that received RIO at the migration and have had no RIO transfer in or out since, so they still hold exactly what they were given. The wallet itself may still be in use: it can trade other tokens or send BNB or ETH. Only its RIO has stayed put.
Dead Wallets: the stricter group inside it. Of those wallets, the ones holding 100+ RIO that have sent no transaction of any kind since the migration on BNB Chain or Ethereum, for RIO or anything else. These are the most likely lost keys, forgotten wallets or deep cold storage. Their RIO is part of the never-moved total, not extra.

Loading transfers

Read daily from public chain data (fetch_large_transfers.py), then topped up every minute by a live check (live-transfers.js) that marks anything newer as new until the next daily run takes it in. BNB Chain, Ethereum and Base are read through the same Alchemy access as the holder counts, because free public nodes keep only part of the history; the script falls back to keyless public nodes without it. The other four chains need no key. Labels are a floor: an unnamed address can still be an exchange we have not identified. Staking, unstaking and reward claims on the native chain are not transfers and are left out. A swap routed through several contracts can show as more than one row in the same transaction. RIO on Algorand and Stellar no longer trades on a live market after the 25 August theft (incident report), so moves there are listed for completeness.
