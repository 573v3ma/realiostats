Independent & good-faith. realiostats is a community-led project, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Realio team. Every figure is drawn from public on-chain data, updated daily, and can be checked. Circulating supply can be measured in more than one legitimate way; this is one transparent, reproducible approach (methodology). Only the Realio team holds the complete bridge records and can publish a definitive number, an invitation we extend openly. Not financial advice.
