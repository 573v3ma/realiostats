Who holds RIO, and how much of it can actually be sold

Circulating supply says how much RIO exists. These two sections say where it sits: which venues hold the tradable float today, and how many wallets hold RIO on each of the seven chains.

Where the liquidity sits liquid float

Circulating supply says how much RIO exists. It does not say how much can be sold right now. Almost all real exchange and DEX volume settles on BNB Chain and Ethereum, so that is where the tradable float actually lives. Here is who holds it.

Wallets holding RIO on each EVM chain, month by month since the 30 October 2024 migration, when the BNB and Ethereum tokens were re-issued on their current contracts. Each point is the on-chain holder distribution at that month's end, netted from the full ERC-20 transfer history. The current contracts carry no history before the migration, so the series starts there. Base starts later, at its first transfer in September 2025, and sits low on this scale because its holder base is small. BNB Chain and Ethereum tell opposite stories: BNB's base has grown, Ethereum's has consolidated from its migration peak. The bold line sums the EVM chains shown for the selected tier, the global trajectory. Algorand, Stellar and Solana appear in the table above but not here: their assets date from 2020 and reconstructing a monthly series would mean walking years of transactions, so they are read as a current snapshot only.

Realio Native wallets by total position, each wallet's liquid + staked RIO, counted daily. Most native RIO is delegated and pooled in the staking module, so a liquid-only count would hide real stakers; we add each wallet's stake across every validator and exclude module/pool accounts. Staked uses the network's bonded stake (1:1 bond weight), the same figure the official Realio validator pages show; because Realio staking is multi-denom, that bundles native RIO with RST and a bridged token. Unlike the EVM chains, native history cannot be reconstructed, so it is counted forward only, from 3 August 2026 when monitoring began, and grows one point per day.
