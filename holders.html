Holder base

How many wallets hold RIO, and how many hold a meaningful amount, per chain. A widening base of holders is a sign of distribution and adoption. Counts are addresses per chain, so a person holding on two chains is counted on each.

Wallets holding RIO on each EVM chain, month by month since the 30 October 2024 migration, when the BNB and Ethereum tokens were re-issued on their current contracts. Each point is the on-chain holder distribution at that month's end, netted from the full ERC-20 transfer history. The current contracts carry no history before the migration, so the series starts there. Base starts later, at its first transfer in September 2025, and sits low on this scale because its holder base is small. BNB Chain and Ethereum tell opposite stories: BNB's base has grown, Ethereum's has consolidated from its migration peak. The bands stack, so the top edge is the combined total across the chains shown for the selected tier. Realio Native sits at the bottom. It has only been measured since August 2026 (native history cannot be rebuilt backwards), so before that its band is held at the first reading and drawn lighter: a placeholder for scale, not a measurement. Native counts each wallet's liquid plus staked RIO. Algorand, Stellar and Solana appear in the table above but not here: their assets date from 2020 and reconstructing a monthly series would mean walking years of transactions, so they are read as a current snapshot only.
